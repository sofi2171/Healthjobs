1. About This Policy

Welcome to Health Jobs Portal (healthjobportal.com, also called “Health Jobs”, “we”, “us” or “our”). We are based in Nankana Sahib, Pakistan, and we serve healthcare professionals and healthcare employers across Pakistan. This Privacy Policy explains what personal information we collect, why we collect it, who can see it, and what choices you have.

It applies to our website, our mobile app, our emails and notifications, and our other services (together, the “Site”). It should be read together with our Terms of Service. By using the Site, you confirm that you have read this policy.

Our services include healthcare job listings from across Pakistan (some posted by employers directly and some collected from other sources), employer accounts, user profiles, direct communication between users, and health and medical news with a focus on Pakistan. You do not need an account to read our news or browse job listings.

2. Information We Collect

2.1Account Information

When you create an account we collect your email address and the password you choose. You can also sign in with your Google account, in which case Google shares basic details with us, such as your name, email address and profile picture. We use your email address to verify your account by sending you a one-time password (OTP). Sign-in and password handling are provided by Google Firebase Authentication.

2.2Job Seeker Profile Information

If you use the Site as a job seeker, we collect the details you add to your profile, such as:

  • Your name and any profile picture you add.
  • Your address or city, so that we can show you jobs near you.
  • Your qualifications and other professional details you choose to add.
  • The kind of work you are looking for, such as the type of job and working hours or shift (for example full-time or part-time).
  • Contact numbers you choose to add or share.

2.3Employer Information

If you register as an employer, we collect details about you and your organization, such as the name of the hospital, clinic, pharmacy, laboratory or company, how long it has been operating, whether it is registered, its address and contact details, and other business details that help us confirm that the employer is genuine (see Section 6). We also collect the job posts you publish, including any photos, documents or videos you upload with them.

2.4Messages and Calls

When you use our built-in chat or calling features to contact another user, we process the messages you send and the technical information needed to connect the conversation, so that messages can be delivered and shown to you and the other person. If you instead contact someone through WhatsApp or a normal phone call, that communication takes place on those services and is governed by their own policies.

2.5Location Information

With your permission, we access your device’s location to show healthcare jobs in your city or area first. Sharing your location is optional. You can turn it off at any time in your browser or device settings, and you can still use the Site without it.

2.6Usage and Device Information

We automatically collect information about how you use the Site, such as the pages, jobs and news you view, the device, browser and operating system you use, and your approximate location based on your IP address. This is collected through cookies and analytics tools (see Section 8).

2.7Messages You Send to Us

If you contact our support team through WhatsApp or email, we keep your message, your contact details and our replies so that we can help you and handle complaints or requests.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use your information to:

  • Create and manage your account, verify your email address and keep your account secure.
  • Show you relevant healthcare jobs, including jobs in your area, and show employers suitable candidates.
  • Let job seekers and employers view each other’s profiles and contact each other for hiring and professional networking.
  • Review employer accounts and job posts to keep listings genuine, and to prevent fraud, spam and misuse.
  • Send you account emails, job alerts and notifications (see Section 7).
  • Respond to your messages, reports and support requests.
  • Understand how the Site is used, fix problems, and improve our jobs, news and features.
  • Show advertising that helps us keep the Site free (see Section 8).
  • Comply with the law and protect the rights and safety of our users and our service.

4. Who Can See and Receive Your Information

Health Jobs does not sell or rent your personal data to third parties.

We share information only in the following situations:

  • Other users of the Site. Profiles on the Site are visible to other registered users. This means job seekers and employers can view each other’s profiles. Anything you put in your profile or job posts, including contact details you add, may be seen by them. Please do not add information you do not want others to see.
  • People you contact. When you chat, call or message another user, they receive what you send and the details you choose to share.
  • Service providers. We use trusted providers to run the Site, such as Google Firebase for authentication and data storage, analytics services, and advertising partners described in Section 8. They handle data only to provide their services.
  • Legal reasons. We may disclose information when the law requires it, when a lawful request is made by a competent authority, or to protect our users, our service or our legal rights.
  • Change of ownership. If the Site is ever transferred or its operation changes hands, your information may be transferred as part of that change, and we will make sure this policy continues to protect it.

5. Applying for Jobs

Health Jobs Portal does not submit job applications on your behalf. The apply or contact button on a job takes you either to the website of the original source or employer, where you apply directly, or to a WhatsApp, phone or chat contact through which you speak to the employer and send your documents yourself.

Once you leave the Site or contact an employer or source, the information you give them is handled under their own privacy practices, and we do not control how they use it. Please share only the information that is needed for the application, and be careful before sending sensitive documents such as copies of your identity card.

6. Employer Verification

To help keep job posts genuine, employer accounts are reviewed and approved by us personally before they can post. We use the business details you give us, such as your organization’s name, how long it has been operating and whether it is registered, only to decide whether to approve your account, to prevent fraud, and to protect job seekers. We may contact you to ask for more information.

7. Emails, Notifications and WhatsApp Channel

  • Account emails. We send emails that are needed to run your account, such as verification codes (OTP) and notices about your account or any request you make, including a request to delete your account.
  • Job alerts. We may send you job alerts and updates by email. You can ask us to stop these emails at any time by contacting us.
  • Push notifications. If you use our mobile app, you may receive notifications, for example about messages or calls. You can turn them off in your device settings.
  • WhatsApp channel. We also post job alerts on our WhatsApp channel. If you follow the channel, WhatsApp’s own terms and privacy policy apply to your use of it. You can leave the channel at any time.

8. Cookies, Analytics and Advertising

8.1Cookies

Health Jobs uses cookies and similar technologies to improve your experience. They help us remember your login session and preferences. You can disable cookies in your browser settings, although some parts of the Site may not work correctly without them.

8.2Analytics

We use Google Analytics and Ahrefs Analytics to understand how visitors use the Site. These services may use cookies or similar technologies and collect information such as the pages visited, device type and approximate location. We use this data in combined form to improve our content and services.

8.3Google AdSense and Third-Party Advertising

We use Google AdSense and other third-party vendors to show advertisements on the Site.

  • Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this or other websites.
  • Google’s use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads based on your interests.
  • You can opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings, or learn more at aboutads.info.

9. Where Your Data Is Stored, Security and Retention

9.1Where Your Data Is Stored

Health Jobs Portal is operated from Pakistan and lists jobs in Pakistan. Our service providers, such as Google Firebase, Google Analytics and Google AdSense, may store or process data on servers outside Pakistan. By using the Site you understand that your data may be processed on such servers, and we take reasonable steps to protect it.

9.2Security

Your data is stored using Google Firebase (including Firestore and Authentication), and we use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect it. Please also protect your account by using a strong password and not sharing it. No method of storage or transmission over the internet is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

9.3How Long We Keep Information

We keep your information for as long as your account is active or as needed to provide our services. When your account is deleted, we remove your account data unless we need to keep some of it for legal reasons, to prevent fraud, or to resolve disputes. Copies of information you already shared with others, such as messages received by another user, or information held by third parties, may remain with them.

10. Your Choices and Rights

  • Access and update. You can view and update your profile information through your account, or ask us to help.
  • Delete your account. You can ask us to delete your account and personal data by sending a request to our support team by WhatsApp or email (see Section 14). We will delete your account and let you know by email.
  • Emails and notifications. You can ask us to stop job alert emails, or turn off app notifications in your device settings.
  • Location. You can withdraw location permission in your browser or device settings.
  • Advertising. You can opt out of personalized ads as explained in Section 8.3.
  • Other rights. You may have additional rights under the applicable laws of Pakistan, such as objecting to certain uses of your data. Please contact us and we will consider your request in good faith.

11. Children’s Privacy

Health Jobs is intended for adults seeking or offering healthcare employment, and it is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has given us personal data, please contact us and we will delete it.

12. Third-Party Links

The Site contains links to other websites and services, including job source pages, news sources and platforms such as WhatsApp. These have their own privacy practices, and we are not responsible for them. We encourage you to read their policies before sharing your information.

13. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time, for example when we add new features. When we do, we will change the “Last Updated” date at the top of this page. Please review this page from time to time. Continued use of the Site after a change means you accept the updated policy.
